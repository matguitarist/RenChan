Mods and walkthroughs, and FSR upscaling.

- Add mods and walkthrough mods to a game: drop a zip, a folder or .rpy/.rpa files on its Mods section. They go in RenChan's copy, never in the game.
- Switch each mod on or off and set their order; RenChan tells you when two mods change the same file or screen, and which one is used.
- Modded play keeps its own saves, and your saves are backed up before the first modded launch.
- Add a mod once, such as Universal Ren'Py Mod, and put it on any game from Add from Library.
- New screen filters FSR Upscale and FSR Sharpen (AMD FidelityFX FSR 1): games made for 720p look sharper in fullscreen.
