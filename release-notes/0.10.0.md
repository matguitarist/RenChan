## Choose the Ren'Py a game plays on, and safer modded saves

- Pick which Ren'Py a game plays on from its details: Automatic, the version it was made with, or another one you have. Try Exact Version can now be undone.
- Copy a game from another disk without RenChan freezing, with progress under the cover and Cancel Copy. A copy cut short is never played.
- Press Play while another Ren'Py downloads: the game starts as soon as its own Ren'Py is in.
- Pick or switch a filter while the game plays on Ren'Py 7.4.6 to 8.1, as on newer versions.
- Saves made with mods are kept: removing a game puts them in the Trash, a backup of them restores into them, and a game update keeps your mods' settings.
- Stopping a game no longer shows an error or counts as a failed run.
