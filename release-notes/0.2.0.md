RenChan's first release: a library for your Ren'Py visual novels that plays them on this Mac, including Windows- and Linux-only releases, without changing the game.

- Drop a game folder, a Mac .app or a zip: RenChan reads its Ren'Py version, downloads the matching official Ren'Py from renpy.org, and plays it from its own copy. If a game crashes, you see Ren'Py's error and can try the game's exact Ren'Py version.
- See your playtime and when you last played, and give each game a status, a rating, labels and notes, then filter the library by them.
- Match a game to VNDB for its cover, developer, release date and description, or choose your own cover.
- Back up a game's saves with one click and restore any backup; your current saves are backed up first.
- Update a game to its new version and keep everything above.
- Discover Ren'Py games on VNDB and open the pages where they're sold or downloaded. In English and French.
