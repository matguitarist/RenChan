Know when your games have a new version.

- Watch a game's itch.io page or F95zone thread: RenChan checks it once a day and shows "New 1.1" on the game's card, with a notification, when a newer version is out. Game › Check All for New Versions (⇧⌘K) checks now.
- Each game shows its own version, and Update to New Version… keeps it current.
- Import your F95Checker library (File › Import from F95Checker…): its games on this Mac come in, each watching its F95zone thread. F95Checker's files are only read.
